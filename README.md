# Orb (beta)

**Show it. Say it. Pass it on.** Orb is a macOS menu bar recorder for giving an AI assistant the context behind your work. Press a shortcut, talk through what you see, and hand off the video, narration, and app timeline.

## Download

**[Download Orb for Mac](https://github.com/nicolaerusan/orb-releases/releases/latest/download/Orb.dmg)**

Requires macOS 26 (Tahoe) or later on a Mac with Apple silicon. Summaries need Apple Intelligence turned on.

## Install

1. Open `Orb.dmg` and drag **Orb** into **Applications**.
2. Open Orb from Applications. It lives in the menu bar as a small open ring.
3. Click **Choose display**, then **Enable screen recording…**, and allow Orb in System Settings. macOS may ask you to reopen Orb.
4. Press **Shift–Command–1** to start recording. Press it again to stop.

## Updates

Orb checks for updates every few hours and downloads them in the background. When one is ready, the menu bar panel shows a **Restart** button; Orb never restarts on its own. To check now, or to turn off automatic downloads, open **Settings → Updates**.

## Feedback

This is an early beta. [Open an issue](https://github.com/nicolaerusan/orb-releases/issues/new) describing what happened, and include your Orb version (shown in the Orb menu). Issues here are public, so leave out anything private from your recordings.

## Privacy

Recordings stay on your Mac in `~/Movies/Orb` unless you turn on cloud sharing. Transcription and summaries run on your Mac.
